All frequently asked questions →
Where should I start my Columbia-area home search?+

Start with your budget, daily destinations, and the kind of home you want. Compare Columbia, Lexington, Irmo, Chapin, Lake Murray, and West Columbia or Cayce using the area guides, then ask James to help narrow the search.

Can I see current homes for sale?+

Yes. The Search Homes links open James’s brokerage property search, where you can check available listings and current details. Property availability and prices can change; confirm them with James before planning a showing.

How can I find out what my home is worth?+

Ask James for a property-specific market conversation. Recent comparable sales, condition, location, and competing inventory help inform a pricing strategy. An automated estimate is only a starting point.

What is different about buying near Lake Murray?+

Waterfront, water-view, and lake-access properties are different categories. Review access rights, dock and shoreline requirements, water depth, and maintenance for the specific property before deciding.
