THE MIDLANDS / LEXINGTON

Room for your next chapter.

Lexington is a useful starting point for buyers comparing town-center convenience with neighborhoods toward Lake Murray and the wider Lexington County area. The right location depends on your daily routes as much as your floor plan.

Buying a home in Lexington

Compare established subdivisions, newer construction, and homes outside a subdivision. Separate the town limits from a Lexington mailing address when checking services.

Think beyond the front door.

Drive your route along US 1, US 378, or SC 6 during the times you expect to travel. Explore the town center and local parks before deciding how close you want to be.

Details worth a closer look.

Review HOA documents, construction warranties where applicable, and ongoing maintenance. Compare the total monthly cost, including association dues and insurance.

For any property, verify school assignments directly with the district and inspect the home’s condition. Choose a location based on your own needs and firsthand visits.
