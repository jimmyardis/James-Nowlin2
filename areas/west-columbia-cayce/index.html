THE MIDLANDS / WEST COLUMBIA & CAYCE

Across the river. Full of possibility.

West Columbia and Cayce are distinct cities across the river from central Columbia. Explore them separately while comparing access to downtown, river recreation, and the roads you use most.

Buying a home in West Columbia & Cayce

Compare established houses, renovated properties, and newer options by condition and location. Similar-looking homes can have very different maintenance histories.

Think beyond the front door.

Try your bridge crossing at commute time and explore the surrounding blocks on foot where practical. Consider how the home connects to the riverfront and your daily destinations.

Details worth a closer look.

Check municipal boundaries and services, drainage, and flood-zone information for the specific parcel. Proximity to the river is not a substitute for an address-level review.

For any property, verify school assignments directly with the district and inspect the home’s condition. Choose a location based on your own needs and firsthand visits.
