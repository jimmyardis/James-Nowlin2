THE MIDLANDS / LAKE MURRAY

Find your place by the water.

Lake Murray is a regional search, not a single town. Compare shoreline locations around Lexington, Irmo, and Chapin while separating the view you love from the access and ownership details you need.

Buying a home in Lake Murray

Decide whether you want private waterfront, a shared launch, a marina nearby, or simply a lake-area setting. Those choices can change your search considerably.

Think beyond the front door.

Visit the property from both a daily-life and recreation perspective. Think through storage, boat access, exposure to sun, and the drive to work and services.

Details worth a closer look.

Ask about dock permits, shoreline restrictions, water depth, maintenance, and flood-zone information. Verify advertised access and permit status with the appropriate authority before relying on them.

For any property, verify school assignments directly with the district and inspect the home’s condition. Choose a location based on your own needs and firsthand visits.
