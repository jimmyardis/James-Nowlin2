LOCAL QUESTIONS, CLEAR STARTING POINTS

A little context.
A lot less guesswork.

James can help you organize the questions. The agency or provider confirms the answer for your address.

Talk with James↗
Where can I find property records for a Columbia-area home?+

Start with the county where the parcel is located. Use Richland County’s mapping and records directory or Lexington County’s property search and mapping tools. Search by the exact address or tax map number; the mailing city alone may not identify the jurisdiction.

How do I know which utility provider serves my new home?+

Confirm service with the provider using the complete address. This hub links to Columbia Water, Dominion Energy, and Mid-Carolina Electric as starting points, but they are not a complete list of every Midlands provider.

Does a home’s mailing address tell me its school assignment?+

No. Confirm attendance boundaries, enrollment requirements, and any program-specific rules directly with the school district using the exact property address.

Where do I check Lake Murray dock and shoreline information?+

Start with Dominion Energy’s Lake Murray page for lake-management and permitting resources, then ask about the specific property’s records. Verify any advertised access or dock rights before relying on them.

Is South Carolina’s 4% legal-residence figure the property-tax rate?+

No. It is an assessment ratio for a qualifying legal residence, not the final tax rate. Review the relevant county assessor’s current requirements and ask for property-specific guidance.
