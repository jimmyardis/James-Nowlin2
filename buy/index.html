YOUR GUIDE TO BUYING

A home that fits your life.

Start with what matters. Build a plan. Find your next chapter.

01

Start with your priorities

Share your budget, timing, daily destinations, and non-negotiables. Distinguish what you need from what would simply be nice to have.

02

Get your financing picture clear

Talk with a qualified lender about your options and documentation before making commitments. James’s mortgage-finance background can help frame the questions to ask.

03

Explore, compare, and tour

Use the area guides and brokerage search to build a shortlist. Compare the full property, its condition, and the location—not just photographs.

04

Move forward with a plan

Discuss an offer, inspections, deadlines, and closing steps with James and the appropriate professionals. Each transaction has its own terms and timeline.
