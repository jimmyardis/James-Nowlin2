YOUR GUIDE TO SELLING

Your next chapter starts here.

A thoughtful sale begins before the listing goes live.

01

Talk about your goals

Begin with your reason for selling, preferred timing, and what comes next. If you are also buying, discuss both sides of the move together.

02

Understand your home’s position

Review relevant comparable sales, current competition, property condition, and location with James. A pricing discussion should be specific to your home.

03

Prepare to make an impression

Walk through repairs, decluttering, presentation, and photography priorities. Focus on preparation that makes sense for the property and your budget.

04

Evaluate offers and next steps

Compare terms as well as price. Discuss contingencies, timing, and the path from an accepted offer to closing before choosing how to proceed.
